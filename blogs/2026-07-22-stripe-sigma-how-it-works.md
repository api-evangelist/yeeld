Quick SummaryBusinesses generate enormous amounts of payment and transaction data every day. Understanding that data can help improve financial visibility, operational efficiency, and inform better decision-making. Stripe Sigma is designed to help organizations access and analyze payment information directly within Stripe using SQL-based reporting tools.
