Quick SummaryA payment integration can process transactions successfully while still containing architectural, operational, or compliance gaps. A thorough integration review evaluates more than code: it examines payment flows, provider configuration, connected-account onboarding, webhooks, reporting, reconciliation, security, and operational readiness. Businesses often invest significant time and resources into payment implementations, software integrations, and custom workflows.
